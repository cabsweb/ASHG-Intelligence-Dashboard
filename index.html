Three shifts to watch

01

AI moves into evidence workflows

The agenda pairs foundation models and agents with calibration, independent evaluation, and clinical interpretation—not only prediction.

02

Scale meets resolution

Million-person biobanks are converging with long reads, isoform-aware analysis, single-cell data, and functional assays.

03

Translation broadens

Therapeutic target selection, population screening, public trust, global implementation, and policy appear alongside discovery science.
